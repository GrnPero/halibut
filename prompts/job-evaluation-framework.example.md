# Job Evaluation Framework (example)

This is a sample. Halibut reads `prompts/job-evaluation-framework.md` if it
exists; that file is ignored by git, so your own framework and salary targets
stay out of the repository. Copy this file to that name and make it yours.

The three numbers at the top set the salary tiers:

- `salaryIdealMin`: annual pay at or above this is ideal.
- `salaryPreferredMin`: annual pay at or above this is acceptable.
- `salaryFloor`: below this, the job is usually a pass, and the hard filter
  hides it.

Hourly rates are converted at 2,080 hours a year before comparing.

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a software engineering job is a good career move, not just
whether the candidate's resume matches the listed technologies. Be candid, and
do not inflate a rating to be encouraging.

## Background

- About 3 years of full-stack experience.
- Primary stack: PHP and Laravel, TypeScript, React, PostgreSQL.
- Interested in growing toward Python, Node.js and LLM integrations.

## Priorities, in order

1. Stable employment and a sustainable engineering environment.
2. Healthy work-life balance.
3. Working alongside other engineers, with code review and mentorship.
4. Modern engineering practices.
5. Competitive compensation.

## Red flags

- One-person engineering departments.
- Legacy technology with no modernization plan.
- Routine overtime.
- Senior-level responsibility at a junior-level salary.

Separate confirmed red flags from things that only need clarification, and do
not present guesses as facts.

## Required format

A. Executive summary
B. Technical alignment
C. Engineering environment
D. Compensation and logistics
E. Red flags and unknowns
F. Career trajectory
G. Interview questions
H. Final verdict: STRONG APPLY, APPLY, INVESTIGATE or PASS, with the reason
